## 2026-07-19 — observation-design note: no action history / opponent-modeling gap

The V3 observation is near-Markovian for OUR side (discard piles, board,
flags already summarize played cards), but carries zero history of the
OPPONENT's moves — their last attack id and the order/timing of their plays
(passed without attacking, just benched an evo line, burned a draw
supporter) are genuinely hidden information not recoverable from the
current state. Cheap fix when needed: M15-style extra id slots (opp last
attack + last-K opp played-card ids) into the existing embedding pathway,
with the pilot accumulating history by diffing observations across turns.
Deferred deliberately: deck surgery and the retreat imitation gap are the
bigger levers now — trigger this experiment when replay forensics show
losses from misjudging the opponent's next attack.
- iter 3 eval: vs_solver 51.0% (bar 53.0 — still within n=200 noise, no
