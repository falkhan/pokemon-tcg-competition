### Corrected causal chain

Under-play supporters and stadiums (draw / gust / disruption) → slower setup and
no gust to break walls → slower prize conversion (**17.8 turns vs teacher
16.6**) → the faithfully-cloned Dudunsparce engine (5.9 vs 5.6 uses/game;

`docs/M26.md:45` already contained the answer: *"the teacher survives it only
because it converts faster."* **The fix is conversion speed, not a draw guard.**

### Mechanism hypothesis (to be tested in Phase D)

A supporter is one-per-turn and a stadium one-per-game, so both are re-offered
at every prompt of every turn while being correct only rarely: 2,211 supporter
offers carry 166 teacher takes. A per-decision pointer-net cross-entropy
(`rl/plan_iter.py:931-938`) learns the base rate "usually not the supporter" and
is under-confident on what is fundamentally a *timing* decision. Items, which
carry no such constraint, are matched or over-played — consistent with the
hypothesis. Note `state.supporterPlayed` exists in the encoding but only as 1 of
1,708 state scalars (`rl/encoders.py:139-146`), with no option-level interaction
term.

## 2026-07-22 ~16:30 — Phase C: the 0.505 pilot's supporter logic is a PRECONDITION TEST, and we already have half of it

**Answer to the sharpened question.** The 0.505 pilot does not express supporter

| card | predicate (`sample-agent/main.py:416-455`) |
|---|---|
| Boss's Orders | `3200 if plan.target >= 1 else -1` — gust iff the plan names a **bench** target |

**🔴 The finding: we already have half of the gust coupling.** `rl/plan.py:116`
computes `needs_gust = tslot > 0` and it rides in the plan vector at
`encode_plan` slot 18; `rl/plan.py:137` already gates bench-target plan
enumeration on a gust card being in hand. So the plan will not propose a bench
target without Boss's Orders in hand — but **nothing makes the model play the
Boss's Orders once the plan has chosen that target.** The sample agent closes
that loop in one line. We never close it.

That is a precise, one-feature explanation for Boss's Orders at **0.8% offline
/ 0.94% live vs the teacher's 6.5% / 3.5%** — and Boss's Orders is the exact
tool for the wall bleed (kangaskhan+crustle 0.31, n=39) that has no offline
instrument.

- **Turn-ending is structurally last in the sample agent** (score tiers: ABILITY

  **→ Phase E drops in priority; do Phase D first.**

## 2026-07-22 ~16:50 — 🔴 Probe 4 (pre-implementation check): the PLAN BLOCK IS INERT — feature 1 was impossible as designed

Before widening the option encoder I checked that `plan.needs_gust` — the thing
feature 1 was going to multiply against — is actually available. It is not.

**The replay-BC corpus carries no plans at all.** `data/bc_m25_alakazam_v3h`
shards hold 12 columns and `plans` / `plan_cands` / `plan_labels` /
`n_plan_cands` are **ABSENT**. `BCDatasetV3`'s shim (`rl/plan_iter.py:591-597`)
therefore feeds **plan = zeros on every training row** and `plan_labels = -1`,
so the plan-head loss term (`rl/plan_iter.py:934-938`, gated on
`plan_labels >= 0`) receives **zero gradient**. Meanwhile
`submission/main.py:198-204` computes `enumerate_plans` → `score_plans` →
argmax at inference and feeds that non-zero plan into `state_enc`.

The plan columns of `state_enc.0.weight` are *not* zero — mean|w| 0.0126,
L2/col 0.232 against the state block's 0.369 — stale weights inherited through
the warm-start chain from the pre-M24 solver lineage (M15–M21), when plans were
real. So the setup looked like a live train/serve skew.

**Measured it (Probe 4):** scored the val rows with `plan = zeros` (the training
condition) against `plan = a real plan vector sampled from `data/plan_m21a`` (the
inference condition):

| | value |
|---|---|
| top-1 flips when a real plan replaces zeros | **1 / 2800 = 0.04%** |
| val_acc, plan = zeros | 0.668 |
| val_acc, plan = real | 0.667 |

**The plan block is inert.** Good news and bad news:

- *Good:* there is no material train/serve skew — the stale plan weights are too
  small to move the argmax. The plan apparatus is dead compute at inference, not
  a live defect.
- *Bad:* **`plan.needs_gust` cannot drive anything.** It is identically zero on
  every training row, so no gradient can ever teach the net to use it. Building
  `is_gust_card × plan.needs_gust` would have shipped a feature that is always
  zero in training — dead by construction. Caught before implementation, which
  is exactly what the verify-before-consequential-actions rule is for.

**Wider consequence worth recording:** the entire M11–M21 plan apparatus
(`enumerate_plans`, `encode_plan`, `plan_head`, the per-turn plan commit) is
**inert in the current replay-BC lineage**. The shipped agent runs it every turn
and it changes nothing. Any future claim that rests on the plan head must first
re-establish that the plan is live — it has not been since M24 swapped the
solver corpus for replay cloning.

### Phase D re-designed (intent unchanged, source changed)

The sample agent's `plan.target >= 1` is computed by **its own search**, not
learned — so the faithful translation is to compute the precondition **directly
from the observation**, not to route it through our dead plan head. All three
features become pure functions of `obs` at encode time, available identically in
`replay_bc` (which has full observations) and at inference:

1. `is_gust_card × (a benched opponent target beats the active one)` — from the
   `rl/combat.py` primitives already used by `_combat_features`, not from the plan.
2. `is_stadium × no_stadium_in_play` — Gravity Mountain's exact predicate.
3. `is_supporter × not state.supporterPlayed`.

Consequence: changing `encode_option_v2` invalidates the stored `options`
arrays, so the corpus must be **rebuilt** (`replay_bc build`, re-encodes cached
replays — no engine simulation) and the warm start must zero-init the new option
columns. That path already exists: `--init-v3o` / `load_v3h_into_v3o`
(`rl/plan_iter.py:739`) was built for exactly this when M16 widened the option
encoding.

only gets Boss's Orders to 4.0% — it lifts the *class*, so the cards that are
easy to place rise while the one that needs a board condition lags.

**4. 🔴 The headline: features × weighting is super-additive on exactly the cell
the features encode.** Boss's Orders — the campaign's worst cell (M26 measured
it at 2/31 = 0.065 val acc; 0.8% play rate; 4/382 live) — goes
**0.8% → 4.0% (weighting) → 6.7% (weighting + features), against a teacher
6.5%.** Stadium likewise 7.0 → 7.7% (teacher 6.6%). Aggregate 1.07× → **1.02×**,
the closest any arm gets to the teacher. The gust feature is what converts a
blunt class lift into a *conditioned* one — it just needed the weighting to
supply a gradient first. That is the mechanism the sample-agent forensics
predicted (`plan.target >= 1` gating the gust) landing on the number it
predicted.

**5. The cost is ATTACH fidelity, and it is real.** control 0.459 → kind 0.287
→ both 0.325. M26 calibrated ATTACH run noise at ±5–10pp on this 157-row class;
control→both is **−13.4pp**, outside that band. The model reaches for supporters
where it used to attach — a genuine trade, not noise. Partial mitigation: the
shipped agent runs the O1 Telepath override, which forces the Telepath attach
regardless of the model's score (live contested share 82%), so the live cost is
smaller than the offline number suggests — but that is a rationalization, not a
measurement, and it does not cover Basic-P or Enriching attaches.

**6. No turn-discipline regression.** `m27_both` ends its turn on 9.6% of
contested decisions vs the teacher's 12.1% (control 11.7%) and keeps a free
action on 95.3% of teacher-free rows (control 93.4%) — it plays *more*, mildly
overshooting. Headline val_acc 0.662 vs control 0.670: the −0.8pp is the price
of trading aggregate accuracy for the two classes we deliberately upweighted.

### Verdict and what is NOT yet known

`m27_both` is the arm. It puts supporters, stadiums and specifically Boss's
Orders at or near teacher rates for the first time in the campaign, at a
measured cost in ATTACH fidelity.

**Strength is completely unmeasured.** M26's law stands and is the reason this
is not a ship candidate: `m26_bc_attach5` bought ATTACH fidelity monotonically
and bought **zero** strength. Fidelity is not strength, and no offline gate here
has demonstrated live-predictive validity. Phase F (wall/stall opponent +
trajectory-statistic acceptance: turns-to-6-prizes, prize-conversion rate) is
what decides whether this arm is worth anything, and it has not run.
