## 2026-07-31 — E0c label shift (n=200 games/arm, value_solve OFF) + VERDICT

| metric | A0 | A1 | A2 | A3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| labels/game | 64.7 | 59.1 | 60.1 | 63.2 |
| **kill-plan commit rate** | **0.070** | **0.270** | **0.277** | **0.269** |
| plan-null rate | 0.938 | 0.773 | 0.755 | 0.757 |
| derails/game | 0.13 | 0.30 | 0.47 | 0.34 |
| attack-when-available | 0.502 | 0.453 | 0.445 | 0.456 |
| end-when-attack-avail | 0.002 | 0.004 | 0.003 | 0.004 |
| supporter-play rate | 0.203 | 0.257 | 0.253 | 0.241 |

- **THE headline: kill-plan commit rate ×3.9** (0.070 → ~0.27) from the leaf
  fix alone. The corpus the old teacher produced carried committed kill-plans
  on 7% of plan rows; the corrected teacher commits 27%. This is the label
  shift H1 needs — LARGE, not negligible.
- **The bar arms add ~nothing to label density on top of the corrected leaf**
  (A1 0.270 / A2 0.277 / A3 0.269): in the WIDENED corpus solver the
  riders-under-bar gap (P0b) is rare — the audit's 6/7 gagged overrides was
  live-budget solve_turn on n=18 prompts, not the corpus regime. No M8.1
  flood trap either (labels/game did not inflate on permissive bars).
- attack-when-available went DOWN (0.502 → ~0.45), against the naive H1
  arrow. Interpretation (recorded before anyone overfits to it): committed
  multi-action prize lines spend prompts on combo assembly (item → attach →
  attack) while an attack sits on the menu — the solver's entire point.
  Attack-per-PROMPT falls while prize conversion rises. The pre-registered
  H1 behavioral predictions proper (sweep share, race conversion,
  prizes/game, attack-when-LETHAL) are measured on the retrained policy's
  gate replays via pm_extra, not on this proxy.
- supporter-play rate UP 0.203 → ~0.25 (the chronic under-play line moves
  the right way); labels/game slightly down = shorter teacher games,
  consistent with actually closing.
- derails/game up mechanically (more committed plans to derail from);
  derail-per-commit roughly flat.
