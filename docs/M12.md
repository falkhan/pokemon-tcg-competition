**Observations / the campaign-level conclusion:** distillation fidelity was
never the problem — the ranker reproduces the widened search's preferences
almost perfectly, and they still lose. Combined with M8.1 (dev-tier override
0.314) and M11 (EI rounds flat below the BC clone), this triangulates ONE
bottleneck: **`score_leaf`'s development scoring is not an improvement signal
on non-lethal turns.** Search depth, distillation quality, exploration
schedules, and student-state coverage all sit downstream of it; no recipe
that consumes score_leaf can beat greedy on the 93% of turns without prize
lines.

**Measured dead ends (add):** distilling dev-aware search preferences into a
confident override (this probe); any improvement operator built on
score_leaf's non-lethal ranking.
